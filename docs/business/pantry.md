# Pantry

- **P1** An **item** is something the kitchen keeps in stock: a name, a **kind**, the **place** it lives in, the **unit** it is counted in, a stock and a "running low" threshold. The kind is **ingredient** (what recipes, meals and preps use; the default, `kind: "ingredient"`) or **household item** (P17). An ingredient lives in the fridge, the freezer or the pantry; a household item always lives at **home**, whatever place is sent (an ingredient sent at home goes to the pantry). There is one place per item and one stock per item. — `Item`, `Kind`, `Location`
- **P2** Quantities are counted in whole **units** and **fractions of a unit**. The unit is only a label: piece, bottle, jar, pack, bag, box, carton, can or tub. There are no grams or millilitres, and pieces take fractions too (½ lemon). — `Unit`
- **P3** A quantity is stored as an integer count of **twelfths** of a unit, so that ¼ ⅓ ½ ⅔ ¾ (3, 4, 6, 8 and 9 twelfths) add up and compare exactly: three thirds are exactly one unit, never 0.999. The API carries that integer (`33` is 2¾). A quantity goes from 0 to 999 units (0 to 11988 twelfths) and anything outside is refused; ⅛ cannot be represented. — `Quantity`, `QuantityOutOfRange`
- **P4** The screen shows a quantity with a fraction glyph where one exists (¼ ⅓ ½ ⅔ ¾ ⅙ ⅚, after the whole number: 2⅓) and `≈0.4` otherwise, with the decimal separator of the language. A piece is shown without its unit; every other unit follows the quantity, in the singular up to one in English and below two in French, in the plural otherwise (zero is plural in English, singular in French). — `quantity.js`
- **P5** The stock never goes below zero. Taking more than what is there (a "one less" tap, a change by a negative amount) empties it and is never refused, because the stock is an estimate. Going above 999 units is refused. — `Item::adjust`, `Item::consume`
- **P6** An item is **running low** when its stock is at or below its threshold, and **out** when the stock is zero (an item that is out is also running low). The threshold defaults to one piece for pieces and a quarter of a unit for every container, also when the unit changes and no threshold is given; it can be set from zero to 999 units. The **stock alert** can be turned off (`alert: false`, on by default) for what the kitchen never keeps, such as a sauce made fresh for each recipe: such an item is never running low, so it is never flagged nor suggested (G4), and it still shows as out when its stock is zero. — `Item::isRunningLow`, `Item::isOut`, `Item::alertWhenLow`, `Unit::defaultLowAt`
- **P7** An ingredient can be **tracked by hand** (olive oil, spices): cooking never deducts it automatically. Such an ingredient, like a recipe line that only says "some", is covered by any stock above zero; every other line is covered when the stock reaches the amount (see [cooking.md](cooking.md) C4). — `Planner`
- **P8** Writes that two phones may send at once are **relative**: the "one more" and "one less" buttons send `adjust by ±12` for the whole units they moved. Taps on the same item are applied on screen at once and merged into a single request once the person has paused for about 400 ms; if the request fails the screen goes back to the stored stock and says so. Only an explicit "set the exact amount" (`SetStock`) and an edit of the item send an absolute value, because someone who has just looked at the bottle is right. — `AdjustStock`, `SetStock`, `useStockAdjustments`
- **P9** An item name is trimmed, has 1 to 60 characters and is unique **ignoring case** (`Milk` and ` milk ` are the same name; `Œufs` and `œufs` too). An item may be renamed to another capitalisation of its own name. The same rule applies when adding and editing. — `ItemNameTaken`, `EmptyItemName`, `ItemNameTooLong`, `ItemRepository::named`
- **P10** A new item starts with the stock given when it is added (`stock`, 0 to 11988 twelfths, none given means 0); the add sheet proposes one unit and lets the person pick the whole number and a fraction, so "what I have and how much" is one step. Editing an item (name, kind, place, unit, hand tracking, threshold) never touches its stock, and the edit sheet and `PUT /api/items/{id}` have no stock. — `AddItem`, `EditItem`, `ItemSheet`
- **P11** Removing an item deletes it with its stock. An ingredient that a recipe line uses, or that a prep makes, is refused instead (422, the message lists the recipe names) until those recipes no longer use it; the meals already logged keep a snapshot of its name and unit (see [cooking.md](cooking.md) C7 and C11). — `RemoveItem`, `ItemInUse`
- **P12** The pantry lists every item **alphabetically**: by name, ignoring case and accents, with a letter and its accented forms together and a ligature spelt out (`épices` with `e`, `œufs` as `oeufs`, with `o`); every list ordered by name (recipes, members, eaters, grocery items) follows the same order (`Alphabetical`). On screen the pantry is split by kind with the Ingredients / Household control: ingredients are then filtered by place with the Fridge / Freezer / Pantry / All control and grouped by place household items form one list whatever their place, and an item added from a tab starts as that kind; items that are out sit dimmed at the bottom of their place. Typing in the search field filters across all places, and when no item has exactly that name it offers to add it. — `ItemQueries`, `PantryPage`
- **P13** The kitchen is shared: everyone who is signed in sees and edits every item (see [accounts.md](accounts.md) A3). An unknown item answers 404, a broken rule 422. — `NotFound`, `DomainExceptionListener`
- **P14** Known limit: there are no row locks, so two requests that change the same item within milliseconds can still lose one change, and the last absolute write wins. This is accepted for two people; an `AtomicChange` with a row lock is the upgrade path.
- **P15** An ingredient is **bought in** some months, all twelve by default (`months`, 1 to 12, in any order, each counted once). Outside those months it is never suggested for the groceries (see [groceries.md](groceries.md) G4), and with no month at all it is never suggested: that is how the kitchen stops buying something. It can still be added to a list by hand, and its stock and recipes are not affected. The month is the server's current month, since the season does not depend on the hour. — `Months`, `Item::isBoughtIn`
- **P16** An ingredient can be a **variant of** a main ingredient (frozen zucchini of zucchini): the main ingredient and its variants form a family, and any of them can stand in for another in a recipe line (see [cooking.md](cooking.md) C4). Variants go one level deep: a variant's main ingredient is not a variant itself, an ingredient that has variants cannot become one, and no ingredient is a variant of itself (422). Deleting the main ingredient leaves its variants as ingredients of their own. Each one keeps its own stock, place, unit, threshold and months. — `Item::makeVariantOf`, `Item::family`, `NestedVariant`, `VariantOfItself`
- **P17** A **household item** (toilet paper, dish soap) is an item of the kind `household`: it is kept like an ingredient, with a stock, a unit and a threshold, and it always lives at home (P1). It is always **tracked by hand** (P7) and **bought all year** (P15, whatever months are sent), and it has **no variants** (P16): it cannot be a variant nor have one (`HouseholdVariant`, 422), so its sheet has no place, no hand-tracking switch, no months and no "Variant of". It is listed, suggested, shopped and put away like any other item (see [groceries.md](groceries.md)), and it **never mixes with the ingredients**: it cannot be a recipe line, the output of a prep, a meal line or an amount used by a prep (`NotFood`, 422), and the recipe, meal and prep pickers (`ingredientsOnly`) never offer it and only create ingredients. An ingredient used by a recipe cannot become a household item (`HouseholdItemInRecipe`) until no recipe uses it. The kind, not the place, decides all of this: an ingredient kept at home is still an ingredient. — `Kind::Household`, `Item::isHousehold`, `ItemSheet`, `ItemPicker`
- **P18** The Pantry page shows a chip with the number of items suggested for the groceries (G4), "N running low", that leads to them. — `PantryPage`
